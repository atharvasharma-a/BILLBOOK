# BILLBOOK

### A simple daily expense tracker designed for students. 

---

## 📌 About the Project

BILLBOOK is a web-based personal expense tracking application designed specifically for students and young adults.

Managing small daily expenses can become difficult when spending happens across different categories such as food, transportation, shopping, entertainment, subscriptions, and college-related expenses. Many existing financial applications provide features that are more suitable for detailed personal finance management rather than quick everyday tracking.

BILLBOOK focuses on keeping expense management simple, quick, and easy to understand.

Along with personal expense tracking, the application also provides a shared-expense feature that allows users to manage expenses with friends, similar to the basic concept of applications such as Splitwise.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students frequently make several small payments throughout the day. These may include:

- Food and drinks
- Transportation
- Shopping
- Entertainment
- College expenses
- Subscriptions
- Bills
- Shared expenses with friends

Because these transactions are often small and frequent, they are easy to forget or ignore.

Existing expense management applications can also feel complicated because of excessive financial features, complex interfaces, or features that are not particularly useful for students.

BILLBOOK aims to solve this problem by providing a simple interface where users can:

1. Record their daily expenses.
2. Monitor their monthly spending.
3. Set and track a basic budget.
4. Understand their spending patterns.
5. Track money received or added.
6. Manage shared expenses with friends.

---

## 💡 Project Objective

The main objective of BILLBOOK is to create a simple and user-friendly expense tracking application that encourages users to record and understand their everyday spending.

The application focuses on:

- Simple expense entry
- Easy navigation
- Clear spending summaries
- Basic budgeting
- Useful spending insights
- Student-oriented expense categories
- Shared expense management
- Clean and responsive UI

The goal is not to create a complex financial management platform, but rather an application that a student can comfortably use as part of their daily routine.

---

# 👥 Target Users

BILLBOOK is primarily designed for:

- College students
- University students
- Young adults
- Students living in hostels or away from home
- Students receiving monthly allowances
- Users who want a simple personal expense tracker
- Groups of friends who frequently share expenses

---

# ✨ Planned Features

## 1. 🏠 Dashboard

The dashboard will provide a quick overview of the user's financial activity.

It will display:

- Current available balance
- Total spending for the month
- Monthly budget
- Remaining budget
- Today's spending
- Recent transactions
- Spending summary
- Simple personalised spending observations

The dashboard is intended to answer the basic question:

> "How am I doing with my money?"

---

## 2. ➕ Add Expense

Users will be able to quickly record a new expense.

Each expense can contain:

- Amount
- Category
- Date
- Payment method
- Optional note

### Expense Categories

Some initial categories will include:

- Food
- Transport
- Shopping
- Entertainment
- Education
- Bills
- Subscriptions
- Other

The expense entry process will be kept short so that users can record an expense within a few seconds.

---

## 3. 📋 Transactions

The Transactions section will display the user's expense history.

Users will be able to:

- View all transactions
- Search transactions
- Filter by category
- Filter by date
- Filter by payment method
- Edit transactions
- Delete transactions

Transactions will be organised in a simple chronological format.

---

## 4. 🎯 Budget Management

Users will be able to set a monthly spending budget.

The application will show:

- Monthly budget
- Amount spent
- Remaining budget
- Percentage of budget used

Users may also define category-wise limits.

For example:

| Category | Budget | Spent |

| Food | ₹3,000 | ₹2,400 |
| Transport | ₹1,500 | ₹900 |
| Entertainment | ₹1,000 | ₹650 |

The application can also calculate an approximate daily spending allowance based on the remaining budget and remaining days in the month.

---

## 5. 📊 Spending Insights

The Insights section will provide simple visual summaries of the user's spending.

Planned insights include:

- Total monthly spending
- Category-wise spending
- Weekly spending
- Average daily spending
- Highest spending day
- Most-used expense category
- Comparison between different weeks

The focus will be on understandable information rather than complicated financial analytics.

---

# 💰 6. Income & Available Balance

Users will also be able to record money received or added to their account.

Examples include:

- Monthly allowance
- Scholarship
- Freelance income
- Part-time income
- Gift money
- Other sources

The application will use the recorded income and expenses to calculate the user's available balance.
